Top Class Education for SC Students

टॉप क्लास एजुकेशन (SC छात्र)

Check Your Eligibility in App

Benefits / लाभ

Full tuition fee + living expenses for SC students admitted to notified top institutions (IITs, NITs, IIMs, medical colleges).

अधिसूचित टॉप संस्थान (IIT, NIT, IIM, मेडिकल कॉलेज) में SC छात्रों को पूरी फीस + रहने का खर्च।

Who Can Apply / पात्रता

  • Category: sc
  • Family income up to Rs. 8 lakh per year
  • Occupation: student

Documents Required / आवश्यक दस्तावेज़

Caste certificate Income certificate Admission proof Aadhaar card Bank account
