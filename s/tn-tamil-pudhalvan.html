Tamil Pudhalvan Scheme

तमिल पुधल्वन योजना

Check Your Eligibility in App

Benefits / लाभ

₹1,000/month for boys who studied classes 6–12 in Tamil Nadu government schools and are now in higher education.

तमिलनाडु के सरकारी स्कूलों में कक्षा 6–12 पढ़े अब उच्च शिक्षा में जुड़े छात्रों को ₹1,000/माह।

Who Can Apply / पात्रता

  • Gender: male
  • Occupation: student

Documents Required / आवश्यक दस्तावेज़

School study certificate Aadhaar card College admission proof Bank account
