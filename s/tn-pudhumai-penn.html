Pudhumai Penn Scheme

पुधुमै पेन योजना

Check Your Eligibility in App

Benefits / लाभ

₹1,000/month paid directly to girls who studied classes 6–12 in government schools, now pursuing higher education (degree/diploma).

सरकारी स्कूलों में कक्षा 6–12 पढ़ी अब उच्च शिक्षा (डिग्री/डिप्लोमा) कर रही लड़कियों को ₹1,000/माह सीधे खाते में।

Who Can Apply / पात्रता

  • Gender: female
  • Occupation: student

Must have studied in Tamil Nadu government/government-aided schools from class 6 to 12; continuing in college.

तमिलनाडु के सरकारी/सहायता-प्राप्त स्कूलों में कक्षा 6 से 12 तक पढ़ी हों; कॉलेज में जारी रखा हो।

Documents Required / आवश्यक दस्तावेज़

School study certificate Aadhaar card College admission proof Bank account
